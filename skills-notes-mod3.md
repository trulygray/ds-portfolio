## Posting 1: Full Stack Developer - AI Trainer

### Required: 
- Knowledge of at least one programming language (Python, JavaScript, C++, Java, etc.).
- Experience with at least one programming framework.
- Ability to review, evaluate, and debug code.
- Strong programming and problem-solving skills.

### Preferred Skills
- Previous professional programming experience.

### Other Requirements
- Bachelor's degree.

## Posting 2: Business Intelligence Analyst

### Required
- Data Analysis and reporting.
- Experience with data visualization tools such as Tableau or Power BI.
- Ability to interpret market, financial, or supply chain data.
- Strong communication and data storytelling skills.
- Ability to collaborate with different teams.

### Preferred Skill
- Programming and scripting experience with Python or SQL
- Advanced Microsoft Excel skills.
- Experience in consulting or specialized industries.

### Other Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in a relevant field.
- 6+ years of relevant experience.
- Master's degree preferred.

## Shared Skills Between Both Postings
- Analytical thinking and problem-solving.
- Ability to evaluate complex technical information.
- Programming or scripting knowledge.
- Identifying errors and improving accuracy.

## Personal Reflection
Something that stood out to me was that both positions require strong problem-solving and analytical skills, even though their responsibilities differ. I also found it interesting that Python can be useful for both AI development and business intelligence. These postings helped me realize how important it is to develop a strong programming foundation for myself, because those skills can apply to a multitude of career paths.
